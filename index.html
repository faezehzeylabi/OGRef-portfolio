01 — Executive Summary

Midwives and obstetricians routinely encounter clinical information gaps that compromise the quality and safety of women's healthcare. To quantify this problem, a structured cross-sectional survey was conducted with 200 midwives and midwifery students across Iran, revealing that 65% rated available clinical resources as inadequate and 18% of those who attempted to find information experienced outright search failure — with gynaecology consultations emerging as the single strongest predictor of unmet need (OR = 3.63; 95% CI: 1.76–7.47; p < 0.001). These findings directly informed the design and development of OGRef, a clinician-led, rule-based mobile decision-support platform for women's health, built through a user-centred process and progressed to a functional published product. A first-author manuscript documenting this two-phase study is in advanced preparation for BMC Medical Informatics and Decision Making, with proposed MPhil research extending this work into clinical decision support and patient safety in high-acuity care.

02 — Project Timeline

Four-Phase Research Arc

Phase 1 — Needs Assessment
✓ Completed
Structured cross-sectional survey of 200 midwives and midwifery students → Quantification of unmet clinical information needs, search failures, and digital tool preferences. Chi-square, Fisher's exact, and binary logistic regression analyses.
Phase 2 — Platform Design & Development
✓ Completed
User-centred translation of survey findings into OGRef — a modular, rule-based Android decision-support platform for women's health. Progressed through incubator acceptance; published to dedicated website; achieved early paid user uptake.
Phase 3 — Manuscript Preparation
⟳ In Progress (80%)
First-author manuscript in advanced preparation for BMC Medical Informatics and Decision Making (Q1) — documenting the full two-phase study with primary quantitative data and platform development methodology.
Phase 4 — Proposed MPhil Research
◇ Proposed
Extension of this translational work into clinical decision support and patient safety research in high-acuity care settings — building on demonstrated competencies in needs assessment methodology, CDSS architecture, and health information systems.
