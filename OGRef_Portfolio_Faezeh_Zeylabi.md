# OGRef: From Unmet Clinical Information Needs to a Digital Decision-Support Platform

**A Research Portfolio for MPhil in Digital Health / Health Informatics**

*Faezeh Zeylabi — Registered Midwife | Clinical Informatics | Digital Health*

## Executive Summary

Midwives and obstetricians routinely encounter clinical information gaps that compromise the quality and safety of women's healthcare. To quantify this problem, a structured cross-sectional survey was conducted with 200 midwives and midwifery students across Iran, revealing that 65% rated available clinical resources as inadequate and 18% of those who attempted to find information experienced outright search failure — with gynaecology consultations emerging as the single strongest predictor of unmet need (OR = 3.63; 95% CI: 1.76–7.47; p < 0.001). These findings directly informed the design and development of OGRef, a clinician-led, rule-based mobile decision-support platform for women's health, built through a user-centred process and progressed to a functional, published product. A first-author manuscript documenting this two-phase study is currently in advanced preparation for *BMC Medical Informatics and Decision Making*. The next stage of this translational work is a proposed MPhil research programme focused on clinical decision support and patient safety in high-acuity care settings.

## Study Overview — Phase 1: Needs Assessment

**Design:** Cross-sectional survey  
**Sample:** 200 participants (88% BSc Midwives; 5% midwifery students; remainder MSc/PhD-qualified)  
**Setting:** Iran

### Key Findings

| Finding | Result |
|---------|--------|
| Rated existing resources as inadequate (Low / Very Low) | **65%** |
| Willing to use a digital clinical decision-support tool | **96%** |
| Willing to pay for such a tool | **38.5%** |
| Experienced search failure (of those who attempted search) | **18%** |
| Identified ease-of-use & rapid access as most important feature | **54.5%** |

### Multivariate Logistic Regression — Predictors of Unmet Clinical Information Need

| Variable | OR | 95% CI | p-value |
|----------|----|--------|---------|
| High need for gynaecology consultation | **3.63** | 1.76 – 7.47 | **< 0.001** |
| Use of educational software (protective) | **0.33** | 0.11 – 0.98 | **0.048** |

*Analysis: Chi-square / Fisher's exact test (bivariate); Binary Logistic Regression (multivariate)*

## Platform Overview — Phase 2: OGRef

**Platform type:** Native Android mobile application  
**Architecture:** Service-Oriented Architecture (SOA); ASP.NET Web API backend  
**Database:** SQL Server (Entity Framework ORM) + MongoDB (activity logging)  
**Decision logic:** Rule-based Clinical Decision Support — static, guideline-aligned pathways (not ML-dependent)  
**Multilingual:** Yes  
**Status:** Functional product published on dedicated website; server infrastructure currently offline; Android APK preserved; content architecture reactivation-ready

### Product Structure

**Three-panel horizontal navigation:**

| Panel | Content |
|-------|---------|
| **Centre (Home)** | 3×3 module grid — unified access to all clinical domains |
| **Right (Specialist Content)** | Articles · Infertility · Perinatology · Gynaecological diseases · Imaging/Ultrasound · Obstetrics & Labour · Gynaecological surgery · Gynaecological oncology · Notifications |
| **Left (Resources & Tools)** | Iranian national protocols · Leitner flashcard system · Terminology · Board & postgraduate exam banks · Differential diagnosis · Paraclinics · Reproductive health (PHC) |
